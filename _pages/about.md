My name is Meizhi Yu, and I am a PhD student in the [Department of Statistics](https://www.stat.uga.edu/) at the University of Georgia, advised by [Prof. Ping Ma](https://bdalpingio.github.io/) and [Prof. Wenxuan Zhong](https://zhonglabuga.github.io/). I received my Master's degree in Biomedical Data Science from Nanyang Technological University and my Bachelor's degree in Statistics from Nankai University.

My research interests include:

* Large Language Models
* Data Selection and Subsampling
* Bioinformatics
* AI for Physical Sciences and Signal Processing
