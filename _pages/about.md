My name is Meizhi Yu, and I am a PhD student in the [Department of Statistics](https://www.stat.uga.edu/) at the University of Georgia, advised by Prof. Ping Ma. I received my Master's degree in Biomedical Data Science from Nanyang Technological University and my Bachelor's degree in Statistics from Nankai University.

My research interests include:

* Statistical machine learning
* Data selection and subsampling
* Bioinformatics
* Large language models
